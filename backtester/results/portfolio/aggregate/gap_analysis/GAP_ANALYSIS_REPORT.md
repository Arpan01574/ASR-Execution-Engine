# Industry-Standard Gap Analysis

This report supplements the main backtest by addressing institutional requirements.

## 1. MAE & MFE Analysis
Maximum Adverse/Favorable Excursion analyzes trade efficiency. Check `mae_mfe_scatter.png`.

## 2. Correlation Matrix
Analyzes the daily return correlation across all 55 symbol/timeframe combinations. Low correlation indicates high portfolio diversification. Check `correlation_matrix.png`.

## 3. Slippage Sensitivity
Evaluates robustness to degraded execution quality. Check `slippage_sensitivity.png` and `slippage_sensitivity.csv`.

## 4. Risk of Ruin
Probability of a 50% account drawdown given the empirical win rate and payoff ratio. Check `risk_of_ruin.csv`.

## 5. Time in Market
Measures the percentage of time capital is exposed to market risk. Check `time_in_market.png`.

## 6. Benchmark Comparison
Compares total portfolio return against a theoretical BTC Buy and Hold benchmark. Check `benchmark_comparison.png`.
